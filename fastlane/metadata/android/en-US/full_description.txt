clipsync is an open-source shared clipboard: copy on one device, paste on another.

It is peer-to-peer with no server, no account, and no cloud. Your devices pair directly
and sync over your local network (and over a Tailscale tailnet). Every clipboard payload
is end-to-end encrypted with a per-pair key before it leaves the device.

Every feature is free and open source. There are no paid tiers and no paywalled features.

Features:

* Text and image (PNG/JPEG) clipboard sync between your own devices
* File transfer: share any file to your paired computer from the Android share sheet;
  files sent from the computer land in Download/clipsync
* Serverless peer-to-peer: no account, no cloud relay, no telemetry
* End-to-end encryption (XChaCha20-Poly1305) with keys derived at pairing (X25519)
* Devices authenticate each other by pinned TLS certificate fingerprint
* LAN discovery over mDNS, plus direct dial of known peers across a tailnet
* Local clipboard history

Android background capture:

Android blocks apps from reading the clipboard in the background. clipsync uses Shizuku
to read and write the clipboard through a privileged shell-uid service, so sync keeps
working while the app is in the background. You need the Shizuku app running and you grant
clipsync access to it once. clipsync never uploads your clipboard anywhere — it only sends
it, encrypted, to the devices you have paired.
